Abstract

HandWorldBench evaluates hand-centric world models across human hands, simulated dexterous hands, and real dexterous hands. From over 600 hours of footage, we curate 20,500 clips and select 500 evaluation references spanning seven task families and 22 task categories. Seventeen metrics assess six dimensions, from visual quality and hand fidelity to physical consistency and temporal coherence.

Evaluating eleven models reveals a gap between controlling hand motion and predicting coherent, persistent changes in the world.
